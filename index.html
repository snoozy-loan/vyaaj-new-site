Questions, answered

The things people ask first

Will checking my offers affect my credit score?
No. Vyaaj uses a soft enquiry to check your eligibility, which is invisible to lenders and leaves your credit score untouched. A hard enquiry only happens later, with your explicit consent, when you choose to formally apply with a specific lender.
Is Vyaaj free to use?
Yes — comparing offers and applying through Vyaaj is completely free for you. We're paid a referral fee by the lender when a loan is successfully disbursed. That fee doesn't change your rate, and because every lender pays us similarly, we have no reason to favour one over another.
How quickly can I get the money?
For pre-approved personal loans, approved funds can reach your account within a few hours. Secured loans like home loans or loan against property take longer because of valuation and documentation, typically a few days to a couple of weeks depending on the lender.
Is my personal and financial data safe?
Your data is protected with bank-grade encryption in transit and at rest. We only share your details with a lender when you choose to proceed with their offer, and we never sell your information to third parties.
Which loans can I compare on Vyaaj?
Personal loans, home loans, business loans, loan against property, education loans and balance transfers — across leading banks and RBI-registered NBFCs. We're adding new lenders and products regularly.
What documents will I need?
To see indicative offers, just a few basic details. To formally apply, lenders typically ask for ID and address proof (Aadhaar/PAN), recent salary slips or bank statements, and for secured loans, property documents. Vyaaj tells you exactly what each lender needs before you start.
