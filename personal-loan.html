Eligibility & documents

What you'll need to apply

Criteria vary by lender — Vyaaj shows you the offers you actually qualify for, so you're not let down later.

Who can apply

  • Salaried or self-employed, aged 21 to 60
  • Net monthly income of ₹20,000+ (varies by city & lender)
  • Credit score of 700+ for the best rates
  • Indian resident with a stable income source
  • At least 1 year of work experience or business vintage

Documents needed

  • Identity & address — PAN and Aadhaar
  • Income proof — last 3 salary slips, or ITR for self-employed
  • Bank statements — last 6 months
  • Employment proof — offer letter or business registration
  • A recent passport-size photograph

Good to know

Personal loan questions

Will comparing offers affect my credit score?
No. Vyaaj uses a soft enquiry to find your matched offers, which leaves your credit score untouched. A hard enquiry only happens with your consent when you formally apply with a chosen lender.
How quickly can I get the money?
For pre-approved offers, approved funds can reach your account within a few hours. For most personal loans, disbursal happens within 24 to 48 hours of document verification.
Are there foreclosure or prepayment charges?
It depends on the lender. Some charge 2%–5% of the outstanding amount, others allow part-prepayment for free after a few EMIs. Vyaaj shows these terms on each offer so there are no surprises.
Can self-employed people apply?
Yes. Self-employed professionals and business owners can compare and apply, typically using ITR and bank statements as income proof instead of salary slips.
What decides the interest rate I'm offered?
Mainly your credit score, income, existing obligations and employer profile. A higher score and lower existing EMIs usually unlock the lowest rates.
