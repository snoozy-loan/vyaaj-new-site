Vyaaj helps you compare loans and connect with lenders. To do that well, we have to handle some of your personal data — so we want to be straight with you about what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the control you have. We collect only what we need, we don't sell your data, and we never share it with a lender until you choose to apply.

Who we are

Vyaaj is a digital loan marketplace operated by Trumflux Private Limited ("Vyaaj", "we", "us"). For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Trumflux Private Limited is the Data Fiduciary responsible for the personal data described here. Our registered office is at 626/E, HSR Jupiter, 1st Main Road, Sector 6, HSR Layout, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560102.

Information we collect

We collect data in three ways: what you give us, what we gather automatically, and what we receive from trusted third parties.

Information you give us

  • Identity & contact details — name, mobile number, email, date of birth and address.
  • Financial details — income, employment type, existing obligations and the loan amount, purpose and tenure you're exploring.
  • Verification documents — where a lender requires them, identity and income proofs (for example PAN, Aadhaar-linked KYC, bank statements or salary slips), shared securely for that application.
  • Anything you send us — messages, support requests and feedback.

Information we collect automatically

  • Device & usage data — IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and actions taken on Vyaaj.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see Cookies & tracking below.

Information from third parties

  • Credit information companies — with your consent, your credit score and report from RBI-licensed bureaus (for example CIBIL, Experian, Equifax or CRIF High Mark).
  • Verification & KYC providers — to confirm the details you provide.
  • Partner lenders — application status and outcome, so we can keep you updated.

How we use your information

We use your data only for clear, stated purposes:

  • To show you indicative loan offers and estimate eligibility and EMIs.
  • To pass your application to the lender(s) you choose, and to follow up on it.
  • To run soft credit checks where you've consented (see below).
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To provide support and respond to your queries.
  • To improve and secure our platform, and to understand which products are useful.
  • To send service communications, and — only where you've opted in — relevant updates and offers. You can opt out of marketing at any time.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and audit obligations.

We rely on your consent for processing such as credit checks and sharing your details with lenders, and on other lawful bases (such as legitimate use and legal obligations) where the DPDP Act, 2023 permits.

Soft credit checks and credit bureaus

When you ask Vyaaj to check your eligibility, we run a soft enquiry with credit bureaus where supported. A soft enquiry lets you see indicative offers and does not affect your credit score.

A hard enquiry — which can affect your score — happens only later, at the lender's end, when you formally submit an application to that lender and authorise it. We'll always make it clear before you reach that step.

When we share your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in these situations:

  • With lenders you choose — we share your details with a bank or NBFC only when you decide to apply to or proceed with that lender. You're never matched into an application without your action.
  • With service providers — KYC, verification, cloud hosting, analytics and communication partners who process data on our behalf, under contract and only as needed.
  • For legal reasons — where required by law, regulation, court order or a lawful request from an authority, or to protect our rights, users and platform.
  • In a business transfer — if Vyaaj or Trumflux Private Limited is involved in a merger, acquisition or restructuring, data may transfer subject to this policy.

Each lender is an independent Data Fiduciary for the data it receives, and its own privacy policy governs how it uses your information once you apply.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, measure how Vyaaj is used, and improve performance. Some are essential for the site to work; others are optional. You can manage non-essential cookies through your browser settings or our cookie controls where available.

How we protect your data

We use industry-standard safeguards — encryption in transit, access controls, and regular reviews — to protect your information. No system is ever completely secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe and to limit who can access it to those who need it. If a data breach occurs, we'll act and notify in line with applicable law.

How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law and regulation (for example, KYC and lending records often have minimum retention periods). When data is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it's processed.
  • Correct or update data that's inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.
  • Erase your data where it's no longer needed and there's no legal reason to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (this won't affect anything done before you withdrew).
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a concern with us first, and escalate if you're not satisfied.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first.

Children

Vyaaj is meant for users aged 18 and above and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has shared data with us, please contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services, technology or the law change. When we do, we'll revise the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, give you a clearer heads-up. Please check back occasionally.
