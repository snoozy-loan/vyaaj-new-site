The idea

One mark, three things it means

The symbol is a “V” for Vyaaj — but it was drawn to carry the brand's whole job in a single, ownable shape. Read it three ways:

Convergence

Two paths meet at a single point — many lenders and offers funnelling down to your best-matched loan. That's the entire function of an aggregator, drawn into one stroke.

A checkmark

The taller right arm reads as a tick — approval, trust, “you're good to go.” Reassurance is the feeling a borrower needs most, so it's built into the letterform.

The match

The mint node is the moment of decision — the single offer that lands. It's the one spot of accent colour in the whole identity, so it always means “this is the one.”

Clear space & minimum size

Give the logo room to breathe

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the symbol (one “×”). Nothing — text, edges, other logos — should enter that zone.

Language

The name carries its own roots

व्याजVYAAJ · “INTEREST”

Because the name is a Hindi word, a Devanagari lockup is part of the system for Hindi-first surfaces, regional campaigns and trust signals in tier-2/3 markets. Keep it the same teal, set in a clean Devanagari face (e.g. Mukta or Hind), and never mix scripts inside a single lockup — show the Latin Vyaaj and Devanagari व्याज as separate, approved versions.
