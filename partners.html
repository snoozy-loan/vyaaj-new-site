Partner questions

The details lenders ask about

How does pricing work?
Vyaaj is performance-priced. You pay a pre-agreed referral fee only when a loan is successfully disbursed — there's no charge for impressions, clicks or unconverted leads. Exact commercials are agreed during onboarding.
How do you keep ranking fair across lenders?
Offers are ranked on what matters to the borrower — rate, eligibility fit and total cost — not on how much a lender pays. Because partners are charged on a similar basis, no one can buy their way to the top. It's the core of how we stay honest.
What integration is required?
You can integrate via REST API for real-time rate and eligibility checks, or start lighter with our partner portal and structured file uploads. We'll work with your tech team to fit your existing stack.
Who makes the final credit decision?
You do, always. Vyaaj matches and refers borrowers; underwriting, approval, pricing and disbursal remain entirely with you and follow your own policies and RBI obligations.
How is borrower data handled?
Borrower information is shared with a lender only when the borrower chooses to proceed with that lender, with their consent, and is handled in line with applicable data-protection law. We don't broadcast a borrower's details to every partner.
