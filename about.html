About Vyaaj

We're here to make borrowing honest.

Vyaaj — from व्याज, the word for interest — is a loan marketplace built on a simple belief: you should be able to see every offer, understand its true cost, and choose for yourself. No pressure, no jargon, no hidden fees.

The problem

Indians overpay on loans — quietly, constantly.

Most people take whatever rate their bank offers, because comparing loans is genuinely hard. Rates are buried, fees are scattered, and the "lowest EMI" rarely means the lowest cost.

Worse, most comparison sites aren't neutral — they push you toward whoever pays them the highest commission. The result is a market that profits from confusion.

We thought that was worth fixing.

✕

Hidden true cost

A low headline rate hides processing fees, insurance and charges that quietly inflate what you repay.

✕

Biased recommendations

Many platforms rank lenders by who pays them most, not by what's cheapest for you.

✕

Fear of the credit check

People avoid shopping around because they wrongly believe every check dents their score.

What we believe

Four principles we won't bend on

They're the reason Vyaaj exists, and the test for every decision we make.

Total transparency

We show the full cost of every offer — APR, fees and total payable — in plain numbers. What you see is what you'll pay.

Always on your side

We rank offers by what's best for you, never by who pays us most. Every lender is treated the same way.

Clarity over cleverness

No jargon, no dark patterns, no fine print designed to trip you up. If a five-year-old couldn't follow it, we rewrite it.

Built for India

Designed for how Indians actually borrow — across languages, cities and income types, with RBI-registered lenders only.

Who we are

Technologists, not bankers. That's the point.

We didn't come from the lending industry, so we don't carry its habits. We came to it as builders and borrowers who were tired of being kept in the dark.

That outsider's view is exactly why Vyaaj works the way it does: we ask what would actually help you, then build it — instead of defending how things have always been done.

We're an India-first team, building in the open, one honest decision at a time.
